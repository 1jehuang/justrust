Install a jr-rustc build (justrust's rustc fork) as toolchain `jr-<commit>`. With no pin, builds then use the newest installed jr toolchain and its proc-macro cache (`JUSTRUST_TOOLCHAIN=system` or `JUSTRUST_CACHE_PROC_MACROS=0` to opt out)

Usage: justrust toolchain jr-install [OPTIONS] [SYSROOT]

Arguments:
  [SYSROOT]  Sysroot with bin/{rustc,rustdoc,clippy-driver,cargo-clippy} [default: ~/justrust/toolchain/rustc/build/host/stage2]

Options:
      --cargo-from <CARGO_FROM>  Installed official toolchain to take cargo and rustfmt from [default: nightly-2026-10-08]
      --no-default               Stage for explicit pins only, without changing the default compiler
  -h, --help                     Print help
