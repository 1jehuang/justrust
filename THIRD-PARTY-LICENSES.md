## unicode-ident 1.0.24

The native lexical scanner uses the `unicode-ident` utility crate, pinned by
Cargo.lock to checksum
`e6e4313cd5fcd3dad5cafa179702e2b244f760991f45397d14d4ebf38247da75`.
Its declared license is `(MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND Unicode-3.0`. This distribution
uses the MIT alternative together with the Unicode data license below. The
crate supplies Unicode XID classification tables, not a Rust compiler or parser.
The pinned tables report Unicode version 17.0.0.
