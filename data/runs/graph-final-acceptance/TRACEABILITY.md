## Next acceptance work, not a completed claim

A native before/after comparison needs two isolated, source-identified release artifacts, the same supported source corpus, fixed worker counts and host conditions, paired repeated check/build runs, exact output checks and failure/resource-profile checks. Report warm/cold conditions and per-case distributions. Such a comparison can support only that native subset workload, not the Cargo session-weighted workload. No fallback to stock may be counted as native success.

The next graph milestone needs a target-keyed admission/body/immediate-successor fact whose evaluation does not await MONO_PLAN or recursively await successors. Cycles, shared successors, unused invalid bodies, generic Drop, source closures, output0 proof gating, and final link completeness must be exercised through check/build and actual native execution before claiming that boundary removed. PARSE/RESOLVE are a separate coordinated lane. Existing full correctness evidence does not make these future requirements complete.
